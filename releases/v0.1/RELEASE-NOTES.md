# Joule Local v0.1 — First community release

A small Android app for controlling the original ChefSteps Joule directly over
Bluetooth, without a Breville account, cloud connection, or Google services.
Designed for the monochrome Mudita Kompakt; requires Android 12 or newer.

Download **Joule-Local-v0.1.apk**, sideload it, allow Nearby devices, then use
**Find Joule**. Press Joule's top button when prompted to complete pairing.
Close other Joule apps before connecting.

Includes pairing, saved keys, live temperature, Fahrenheit target controls,
START / SET, STOP, a phone reminder timer, and diagnostics.

**The timer does not stop the heater. Disconnecting or closing the app does
not stop Joule.**

The first tester reported that the app worked on their Mudita Kompakt and
original ChefSteps Joule. Broader compatibility has not been established;
exact model, firmware, and individual feature results remain unrecorded.
All 33 protocol checks passed, including seven comparisons with upstream.

Protocol credit: [acato/ha-joule](https://github.com/acato/ha-joule), Apache-2.0.
This app is not affiliated with Breville or ChefSteps. Contributions and
hardware compatibility reports are welcome.
